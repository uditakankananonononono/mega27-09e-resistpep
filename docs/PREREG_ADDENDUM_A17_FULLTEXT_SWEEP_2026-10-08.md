# A17 prereg (locked before any A17 search) - full-text PMC sweep for the 09d gate
Extends A15 (abstract-level search, one full text). Same gate criteria 1-5 and same PARTIAL definition as A15 (docs/PREREG_ADDENDUM_A15_GATE_09D_EVIDENCE_SEARCH_2026-10-08.md), unchanged. Polymyxin/colistin counts only as PARTIAL. Mass-spec stays not applicable.
Disclosure: the design is informed by A15's partial findings (Lofton 2013 PMID 23894360 already known; lptC and wzzE had no abstract hits; basR only polymyxin-type). A17 searches full text because abstracts miss gene-level phenotypes. Not blind. No new endpoint: the only output is a per-paper verdict against criteria 1-5 and the gate status.
Source: NCBI eutils, db=pmc (open-access full text only; paywalled and non-PMC papers are invisible, so UNMET here cannot show absence of literature). Fixed queries, each with retmax 200:
Q1 (basS OR pmrB) AND ("LL-37" OR cathelicidin OR CAP18 OR "antimicrobial peptide") AND (Escherichia OR Salmonella OR Enterobacter OR Klebsiella)
Q2 (basR OR pmrA) AND (defensin OR "LL-37" OR cathelicidin OR "antimicrobial peptide") AND (Escherichia OR Salmonella OR Enterobacter OR Klebsiella)
Q3 lptC AND ("LL-37" OR cathelicidin OR CAP18 OR "antimicrobial peptide" OR defensin)
Q4 (wzzE OR "wzz(ECA)" OR "ECA chain length") AND ("LL-37" OR cathelicidin OR CAP18 OR "antimicrobial peptide" OR defensin)
Screen: fetch full text XML of every returned PMCID. Automatic filter: keep a paper only if one paragraph contains (a) one of the gene names basS, basR, pmrA, pmrB, lptC, wzzE (case-sensitive, word-bounded; pmrA/pmrB retained only for Enterobacterales papers), (b) one of LL-37, cathelicidin, CAP18, defensin, hBD3, "antimicrobial peptide", and (c) a perturbation word (mutant, mutation, deletion, knockout, delta, "\u0394", overexpress, knockdown). Then I read every kept paragraph and score criteria 1-5 per paper from the retrieved text. Papers by Spohn et al. and anything reusing Spohn 2019 sequencing data fail criterion 2. All PMCIDs, filter results and verdicts are logged; nothing judged from memory.
Gate: MET only if at least one paper satisfies 1-5 for one of the four genes with a matched host-defence AMP (LL-37/cathelicidin for basS, lptC, wzzE; defensin for basR). Otherwise UNMET, with PARTIAL papers listed. A result of MET still does not make a named discovery; the claim stays descriptive unless the user's rules for named discoveries are separately satisfied.
Limits locked in: OA-only; keyword filter can miss synonyms (e.g. pmrB-reported-as-"PmrB", mixed case, pasted into tables/figures); screened by one reader; no systematic-review claim.
