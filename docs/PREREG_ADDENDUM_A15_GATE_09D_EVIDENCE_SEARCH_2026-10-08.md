# A15 prereg (locked before any search) - what record would meet the 09d gate for the A12 claim
Claim tested: perturbation of basS/basR (PmrB/PmrA-type), lptC or wzzE alters susceptibility to host-defence AMPs in Enterobacteriaceae, matching the Spohn lines' AMP-specific recurrence (basS: CAP18/LL-37-class; basR: HBD3; lptC, wzzE: LL-37).
A record MEETS the gate only if ALL hold: (1) a PubMed-indexed peer-reviewed paper (PMID recorded); (2) independent of Spohn 2019 (different authors/experiment; may cite it but not reuse its sequencing data); (3) a defined perturbation (deletion, point mutation, or targeted knockdown/overexpression) of one of the four named genes (or the Salmonella ortholog confirmed in A11 for basS) in E. coli, Salmonella or another Enterobacterales; (4) a measured change in MIC, survival or killing for a cationic antimicrobial peptide (the same AMP, or the same family, e.g. LL-37/cathelicidin, defensin, polymyxin counted ONLY as a secondary mention, not as meeting); (5) the abstract or full text states the direction of change explicitly.
PARTIAL: criteria 1-3 hold but only polymyxin/colistin or a non-matched AMP was measured; or lptC/wzzE evidence is indirect (LPS structure only, no AMP phenotype). PARTIAL does not meet the gate.
"Exact-peptide mass-spec" is not applicable: this claim names no peptide; a mass-spec record is neither required nor sought here.
Search: PubMed (NCBI eutils esearch + abstract fetch) with gene-name x AMP term pairs (basS, basR, pmrB, pmrA, lptC, wzzE, wzz; LL-37, cathelicidin, CAP18, defensin, hBD3, antimicrobial peptide; E. coli, Salmonella). Every PMID screened is logged with the verdict; nothing is judged from memory. Verdicts per criterion are recorded from the retrieved abstract text. The gate stays unmet if no record satisfies 1-5. Exposure disclosed: I know from background that PmrAB/PhoPQ-mediated lipid A modification affects polymyxin and cationic-peptide resistance; this lock does not accept that as evidence, only retrieved records.
