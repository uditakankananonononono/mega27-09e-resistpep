# A12 prereg (locked before any A12 computation) - sbmA-independent Tier A recurrence
Purpose: after A9 (rule not met) and A10 (no line dependence shown), test whether Spohn 2019 shows within-AMP Tier A gene recurrence beyond length-weighted mutation opportunity in lines that carry NO sbmA deletion. This is an orthogonal support leg for the 09d candidate story that does not rely on sbmA or waaY.
Prior exposure (disclosed): A5/A6 already showed per-gene recurrence counts (e.g. basS 4/10 in CAP18, waaY in BAC5). The aggregate statistic below was not computed before this lock.
Universe: the 28 sequenced adopted-AMP Spohn lines (A10 correction). Events: Tier A records (A6 tier function) of evolved Spohn lines, one event per distinct (line, gene); intergenic and Tier B (large-deletion membership) excluded. Lines in S (sbmA-deletion lines, A10) are EXCLUDED. Genes are mapped by name to the pinned MG1655 CDS table (4,318 CDS); unmapped gene names are excluded and their count reported.
Statistic T: number of (AMP, gene) pairs with Tier A events in >= 3 distinct non-S lines of the same AMP.
Null: for every non-S line keep its number of mapped Tier A events n_l; redraw n_l distinct genes from the 4,318 CDS with probability proportional to CDS length; recompute T. 100,000 replications, numpy default_rng seed 20261012. p = P(T_null >= T_obs).
Reading rule: p < 0.05 -> "AMP-specific Tier A recurrence beyond length-weighted opportunity is present outside the sbmA-deletion lines"; otherwise "not shown". Either way report the contributing (AMP, gene) pairs descriptively.
Limits locked in: the null ignores hotspot and mutation-rate heterogeneity and hypermutator lines; lines within an AMP are not independent; this supports a descriptive recurrence claim only and does not by itself meet the 09d gate (independent exact-peptide mass-spec or PubMed record).
